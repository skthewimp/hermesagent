# Karthik Writing Style

Use this skill whenever the user asks for prose that represents Karthik, especially blog posts, website copy, technical writeups, chart captions, summaries, explanations, professional updates, or public-facing text.

Before producing final prose, read `references/writing-style-guide.md` and apply it directly. The full guide is the source of truth.

Core operating rules:

- Sound like a specific human, not brand copy or generic AI.
- Keep the writing concrete, observant, and lightly opinionated.
- Prefer plain sentences with real texture over polished marketing cadence.
- Preserve uncertainty, specificity, and lived-in context where the draft has it.
- Do not over-smooth the voice into formal corporate prose.
- For technical writing, keep the explanation human and grounded in what actually happened.
- Match the form: short posts need one live observation; mid-form posts need a personal trigger plus one analytical pattern; long-form posts need sectioned development, caveats, and a real landing.
- Keep the typical arc visible: incident, curiosity, analysis, counterpoint, informal close.

When editing existing text:

- Preserve the author's intent and useful quirks.
- Tighten weak phrasing without sterilizing the voice.
- Remove generic filler, inflated claims, and AI-ish transitions.
- Keep jokes or self-deprecation only when they feel earned.
- Keep PS notes, caveats, and small digressions when they carry personality or useful context.
